**Carry-forward item (2026-05-12):** J7 pins 12, 14, 16, 18 are now relay drivers on the Sparrow FE. The Test Adapter must provide:
- Pull-up to 2.5 V on each of the four pins
- Connection to FE_MPIO_[8:11] (one MPIO per relay driver)

Readback behavior: relay driver OFF → MPIO reads 2.5 V (pull-up wins); relay driver ON → MPIO reads 0 V.

Purpose: lets the production test verify the FE relay-driver outputs via Accordion MPIO inputs rather than needing a separate measurement probe.
